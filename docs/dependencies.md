Original legal resources are retained under `third-party/` in the application JAR. The embedded RefactorJ and JUnit JARs also retain their own manifests and license resources. Additional exact bundle provenance and shared license texts are documented in [RefactorJ's audit](../refactorj/docs/LICENSES.md).

After resolving dependencies, regenerate the local inventory and notices with `python3 scripts/collect-notices.py`, then rebuild the application. The script reads `.cache/m2` and does not upload data. Review `runtime-dependencies.tsv`, the POMs and preserved legal files together when upgrading dependencies.
